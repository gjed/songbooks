This project doesn't own any of the songs in it.

Every songbook here is a collection: lyrics, chords, and arrangements found
scattered around the internet — forums, tab sites, old blog posts — or
transcribed by ear from recordings, or handed over directly by the artists
themselves. None of it is original work claimed by this repository.

The music, lyrics, and chords remain the property of their respective
artists, songwriters, and publishers. This project only repackages and
organizes publicly available material into printable songbooks for
personal, non-commercial use — rehearsals, sing-alongs, and the like. No
songbook here is sold or monetized.

Chord transcriptions are inevitably imperfect: ears mishear, memories slip,
and different recordings of the same song don't always agree. If something
is wrong, missing, or you'd simply rather it wasn't here, the fastest way
to reach us is below.
