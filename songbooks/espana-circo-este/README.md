## España Circo Este

Songbook for the Italian band **España Circo Este** (formed 2013, Abruzzo) —
reggae, indie rock, punk and Latin American influences with lyrics in
Italian and Spanish. Ten songs spanning *La revolución del amor* (2015),
*Scienze della maleducazione* (2017), *Machu Picchu* (2020) and
*Canzoni per la decrescita felice* (2025).

Ukulele, English chord notation.
