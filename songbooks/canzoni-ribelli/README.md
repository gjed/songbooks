## Canzoni Ribelli

Italian partisan and protest songs — anthems of resistance and struggle,
passed down through generations of communal singing. Opens with *Fischia il
vento*, written in 1943 by partisan commander Felice Cascione to the melody
of the Russian song "Katyusha"; it became one of the best-known anthems of
the Italian Resistance. Ukulele, Italian chord notation.
